CV

I am a Master's student in political science at Duke University. Before moving to Duke, I earned my Bachelor's degree in political science and economics at Peking University.

Research

My research sits at the intersection of authoritarian politics, causal inference, and computational social science.

Substantively

I am working on how the elite politics of communist parties shape the dynamics of both cooperative and antagonistic state-society interactions in contemporary China.

Methodologically

I am interested in experiment design and analysis, doubly-robust causal learning, heterogeneous treatment effect estimation, and how deep learning algorithms can empower empirical research through structured and unstructured data processing.
